# MGT ’27 Execution Plan · BRENSH

BRENSH’s execution plan for Mindspace Got Talent ’27 at Mindspace Airoli East (Mumbai) and Mindspace Madhapur (Hyderabad), prepared for Mindspace Business Parks REIT stakeholders.

BRENSH executes the programme end to end. The page covers the five phases and who owns each, the participant journey from registration to confirmed finalists, audition-day operations, judging, the bridge from auditions to finale, the recommended campaign plan, both finale nights (run of show, production, capacity, safety and permits), and a week-by-week delivery plan with the decisions needed from Mindspace.
